SmokeFree Note 한국어

A quit-smoking companion that fits in your pocket

SmokeFree Note shows how long you have gone without smoking and how much you have saved, and helps you log cravings and get through them. It is a web app you can add to your phone's home screen.

Open the app

The app screens are in Korean. An English version of the app itself is not available yet — this page only introduces it.

What you can do

  • Today — smoke-free time, cigarettes not smoked, money saved, and a 5-minute “hold on” timer for when a craving hits.
  • Log — record how strong a craving was, your mood, the situation, and what helped; you get one coping suggestion right away.
  • Stats — the last 7 days, and which times of day, situations and moods come up most.
  • Health — a general recovery timeline from WHO and CDC public information.
  • Help — reminders, big-text mode, and export/import of your own records.

Your records

Records are stored on your own device (browser local storage). Optional research participation, if it is ever offered, needs your explicit consent inside the app. Nothing on this page collects any information.

SmokeFree Note is a self-tracking tool. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and is not a substitute for advice from a doctor or pharmacist.
